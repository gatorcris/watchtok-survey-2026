# The 2026 WatchTok Enthusiast Survey


**Production Draft V7 — September 1, 2026**


> This document contains the participant-facing production questionnaire followed by implementation notes. Question wording and answer choices are carried forward from the validated V5 instrument unless explicitly identified as new production copy. Stable V5 question IDs are preserved in Markdown comments for data continuity.

## Welcome


This is a real enthusiast survey—not a three-question engagement poll.


Most participants should finish in about **10–15 minutes**. Please allow up to **20 minutes** if you enjoy thinking about your answers.


Your complete response will help us understand how WatchTok enthusiasts and collectors discover, evaluate, purchase, and enjoy watches.


This research is completely independent of watch brands. No brand has sponsored, commissioned, reviewed, or approved the survey. Watch-industry employees may participate as individual enthusiasts.


The research questionnaire does not ask for your name, TikTok handle, or email address. Responses will be analyzed in aggregate. After completing the survey, you may separately choose to provide an email address to receive the report or hear about a future WatchTok survey.


Please answer for yourself. Some questions ask for one answer, some allow every answer that applies, and a few ask you to choose up to three. Your best reasonable estimate is perfectly fine.


**Ready? Let’s talk watches.**

# Production implementation notes


These notes are for the organizer and survey developer. They are not participant-facing.


## Instrument status


- This is the production-oriented successor to the canonical V5 pilot instrument.
- V7 neutralizes the respondent-facing purpose language to reduce demand characteristics and response priming.
- If stable Q1 is “None,” stable Q2 is skipped and annual watch spending is derived as `$0` during analysis.
- Pilot-only questions F1–F8, participant codes, AI administration rules, provisional profile generation, and raw-response output instructions have been removed.
- The 43 validated research questions remain intact. Stable V5 IDs appear in hidden Markdown comments so the existing codebook and pilot comparisons remain usable.
- Display order now prioritizes collecting behavior and next-purchase outlook before later diagnostic and segmentation questions.
- Q12 is the only dependency-driven exception to the agreed section order. It appears at the start of the TikTok pathway section because its answer controls routing for Q22–Q25 and Q13–Q16.


## Required interface behavior


- Show a visible progress bar based on the respondent’s routed path, not simply 43 total questions.
- Present one question per screen on mobile unless usability testing clearly supports a small grouped set.
- Allow Back navigation without losing answers.
- Support save-and-return if the production platform can do so securely.
- Keep the participant-facing section transitions and completion message in the routed flow.
- Do not display stable V5 IDs or implementation notes to respondents.
- Preserve the listed option order and stored option codes; do not randomize answer choices.


## Validation and routing


- Require an answer to each displayed research question unless the respondent is routed around it.
- Enforce a maximum of three selections for Questions 12, 19, 20, and 21 in this display version (stable IDs Q40, Q23, Q24, and Q25).
- Multi-select “None,” “I am not sure,” and “Prefer not to answer” choices must be exclusive when they conflict with substantive choices.
- Question 1 / stable Q1, option 1: skip display Question 2. Derive annual watch spending as `$0`; do not classify it as ordinary missing data.
- Question 9 / stable Q37, option 7: skip display Questions 10–15.
- Question 16 / stable Q12, option 1: skip display Questions 18–21 and 36–39.
- Question 22 / stable Q26, option 14: skip display Questions 23–24.
- Display Questions 37–39 / stable Q14–Q16 only if display Question 36 / stable Q13 includes option 6.
- Store routed questions as a distinct `SKIPPED` or null-by-routing state, not as ordinary missing data.


## Recruitment attribution


- Capture each Founding Creator Partner’s referral code from the unique survey link or QR code.
- Store referral source as campaign metadata rather than asking the respondent to identify the creator.
- Report each creator’s completion count privately; use a shared public progress goal rather than a leaderboard.
- The coalition goal is **700 completed responses**.
- Keep all public respondent recruitment language neutral. Do not ask followers to prove that creators matter or to demonstrate creators’ commercial value.
- Recommended public invitation: “Help us build a clearer picture of how WatchTok enthusiasts discover, evaluate, and buy watches.” The stronger commercial-value framing belongs only in the private Founding Creator Partner pitch.


## Privacy and independence


- Do not collect a respondent’s name or TikTok handle in the research questionnaire.
- Place optional email collection after the completed-response event and store it separately from research answers.
- Use optional email only to distribute findings or invite participation in future WatchTok research.
- Do not sell or disclose individual responses, email addresses, or individual creator referral performance.
- No watch brand may sponsor, commission, review, approve, or alter the survey instrument or its findings.
- Brand employees and owners may answer as individual enthusiasts. Stable Q20 identifies relevant industry relationships so analysis can be checked with and without industry-affiliated respondents.


## Recall-window and field-period notes


- The questionnaire intentionally uses different recall periods for different behaviors: 12 months for purchasing, sales, events, and industry relationships; 90 days for recent WatchTok participation; and six months for creator output.
- Do not add or compare raw activity counts across different recall windows without explicitly normalizing them.
- Report the survey field dates and retain each respondent’s completion timestamp.
- Stable Q19 measures event attendance over the rolling 12 months preceding each respondent’s completion date. If responses span Windup Watch Fair on October 16, define any pre-Windup and post-Windup cohorts explicitly before interpreting event-attendance differences.


## Interpretation safeguards


- Treat Q37–Q43 as stated next-purchase outlook, not a prediction of an exact purchase.
- Do not infer purchase influence from creator posting volume.
- Do not infer substantive creator depth from wristchecks alone.
- Do not infer commercial value from follower count alone.
- Do not infer small-brand readiness from waiting tolerance alone.
- Do not describe stated preferences in Q23–Q25 as demonstrated conversion effects.
