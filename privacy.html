WATCHTOK SURVEY

Privacy Statement

Effective September 8, 2026

WATCHTOK SURVEY is an independent, creator-led research project designed to better understand how watch enthusiasts discover, evaluate, purchase, and enjoy watches.

Privacy-conscious by design. The 2026 WatchTok Enthusiast Survey collects useful research while minimizing information that could directly identify respondents.

Information collected through the survey

When you participate, we collect:

  • Your answers to the survey questions
  • A randomly generated anonymous identifier
  • Whether your response is in progress or completed
  • The last question you reached
  • The date and time your response was started, updated, and completed
  • The version of the survey being completed

The anonymous identifier allows your browser to save your progress, return to the survey, and update the correct response. It is not your name, email address, TikTok handle, or social-media identity.

The public survey does not use creator-specific links. We are not using the survey to attribute individual respondents or responses to particular WatchTok creators.

Information we do not intentionally collect

The research questionnaire does not ask for or intentionally collect:

  • Your name
  • Your TikTok or other social-media handle
  • Your telephone number
  • Your mailing address
  • Your precise geographic location or GPS data
  • Your advertising identifiers
  • Your activity on other websites

The survey does not use Google Analytics, TikTok Pixel, Meta Pixel, advertising cookies, behavioral advertising tools, or similar tracking technologies.

Saving progress on your device

The survey saves a copy of your answers and progress in your browser's local storage. It also stores an anonymous authentication session that allows the survey to recognize the same browser and securely update its own response.

This information remains on that device and in that browser unless it is cleared through the browser's privacy or website-data settings. Clearing browser data may prevent you from returning to an unfinished response.

Optional email information

After completing the survey, you may separately provide an email address if you would like to be invited to future WATCHTOK SURVEY research.

Providing an email address is entirely optional. The email record contains your email address, your consent to receive future survey invitations, and the date and time of that consent.

Email addresses are stored in a separate database table. The email record does not contain your survey-response ID or anonymous survey identifier, and your email address is not included in your survey answers.

We will not provide optional email addresses to watch brands or participating creators. You may withdraw your consent by contacting watchtoksurvey@gmail.com.

Although the databases do not contain a direct link between an email address and a survey response, no technical system can promise that indirect inference is impossible. For example, records submitted at nearly the same time could potentially be compared, particularly during periods of low survey activity.

Hosting and routine technical logs

The WATCHTOK SURVEY website is hosted through GitHub Pages, and survey responses are processed and stored using Supabase.

As with nearly all websites and online database services, these providers receive technical information needed to deliver, secure, and operate their services. Routine provider logs may include:

  • IP address
  • Browser or device information
  • Request date and time
  • Requested pages or service endpoints
  • Authentication and security events

An IP address, browser type, or device type is not stored as part of your WATCHTOK SURVEY response or optional contact record. However, GitHub and Supabase may retain this information in their own operational or security logs according to their policies.

Additional information is available in the GitHub Privacy Statement and the Supabase Privacy Policy.

How we use survey information

Survey responses may be used to:

  • Analyze watch-enthusiast interests and purchasing behavior
  • Understand how enthusiasts discover and evaluate watches
  • Study the role of creators and online communities
  • Prepare reports, presentations, articles, and other research materials
  • Improve future WATCHTOK SURVEY research

Results may be shared publicly and with creators, watch brands, media, or other interested parties in aggregated or summarized form.

We do not intend to publish, sell, or disclose individual survey records. Optional email addresses will not be included in research results.

Information security

The survey uses encrypted HTTPS connections. Database permissions and row-level security are configured so that a respondent's browser can access only its own survey response and cannot browse other respondents' answers or contact information.

No online system can be guaranteed completely secure, but reasonable technical safeguards are used to reduce unauthorized access or disclosure.

Retention

Survey responses will be retained only as long as reasonably necessary to conduct, validate, analyze, document, and report the research.

Optional email addresses will be retained while the future-research invitation program remains active or until the individual withdraws consent.

GitHub and Supabase maintain their own retention practices for routine operational and security logs.

Your choices

Participation is voluntary. You may stop answering at any time.

You may contact us to ask a question about this statement, withdraw an optional email consent, request deletion of an email address, or request deletion of a survey response.

Because survey responses are not connected to a name or email address, we may be unable to identify a particular response without sufficient information, such as an approximate submission date and time. We will not attempt to identify a respondent merely to fulfill a deletion request.

Information already included in completed aggregate analysis may not be removable because it no longer represents an individual record.

Changes to this statement

We may update this Privacy Statement if the survey, its technology, or its data practices change. The effective date at the top will identify the latest version.
